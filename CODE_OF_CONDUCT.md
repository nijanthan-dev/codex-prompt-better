# Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation harassment-free and welcoming regardless of
identity, background, experience, or viewpoint.

Be respectful, constructive, and focused on the work. Do not harass, threaten,
publish private information, or engage in sustained disruption. Maintainers may
remove content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to
protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub contact
options. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details. Reports will
be reviewed promptly, kept confidential where practical, and handled fairly.

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
